A tool that automatically ports PS5 executables to Linux and Windows.

AnyPS5 is a native PS5-to-PC porter: it relinks PS5 executables into Linux ELF or Windows PE binaries and ships stub implementations of PS5 PRX system libraries so the converted binary can dynamically link without a separate runtime process. It also includes a SPIR-V shader recompiler and SDL controller mapping. Trending on organic interest and the perennial appeal of game preservation milestones — producing a binary that runs natively without emulation is a technically striking achievement. Developers and preservation researchers who supply their own PS5 binaries and want to study or run them on open hardware are the intended audience.
